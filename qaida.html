القَاعِدَةُ النُّورَانِيَّة

Nooraniyah Companion

Lessons 1–10, retyped in the book’s own colours with English labels. Tap any letter or word and its spelling opens right underneath, the way the teacher reads it: letter name, mark, sound, then the whole word.

The symbols

Every mark shown on the letter بـ. In the lesson grids the marks keep these colours, so a blue mark is always a kasra.

How the teacher reads (tahajjī)
  1. Say the letter’s name.
  2. Say the mark’s name (fatha, kasra, sukoon…).
  3. Say the sound it makes.
  4. Move to the next letter. At the end, read the whole word smoothly.

Example, ءَامَنَ: “Hamza fatha a · Alif madd aa · Meem fatha ma · Noon fatha na → aamana.”
